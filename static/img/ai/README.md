# AI brand marks

Used by the chat's "Switch model" dialog to tell providers and models apart.

Source: [lobe-icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.50.0, MIT).
Single-colour marks (openai, grok, ollama, openrouter, kimi) are drawn with `currentColor`
and rendered through a CSS mask so they follow the theme; the rest keep their brand colours.

The marks themselves are trademarks of their respective owners and are shown only to
identify the provider or model family.
