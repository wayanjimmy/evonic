# Support playbook

## Billing
- Duplicate charge: confirm both charge ids, refund the duplicate, then reply.
- Failed payment: never ask for full card numbers; ask the customer to update the card themselves.

## Technical
- Collect product, version, exact error text and reproduction steps before troubleshooting.
- Attach logs with the ticket, not inside the reply body.

## Account
- Ownership transfers and account deletions always need a human.
